Onboarding Missions · MoneyLion

Designing Onboarding Missions to Drive First-Time Activation

Guiding users with purpose-driven tasks to turn early interest into real engagement.

Role
Product designer
Scope
Web and app
Company
MoneyLion
Focus
Day 1 engagement
The problem

New users signed up, then didn’t know where to start.

Goal: help new users experience MoneyLion’s core value quickly with clear, action-driven onboarding missions, to boost Day 1 and Day 7 engagement, increase first-time deposit setup, and improve product discovery and feature engagement.

  • 01

    New users were dropping off after sign-up without exploring MoneyLion features.

  • 02

    Users didn’t know what to do next or how to unlock value quickly.

  • 03

    Early tasks lacked incentives or feedback, making the experience passive.

  • 04

    Limited visibility into what actions drove long-term retention.

Strategy

Turn passive exploration into rewarding progress.

New users often dropped off after sign-up, unsure of what to do next. To reduce early churn and drive early wins, we introduced guided missions.

  • What we introduced

    • Actionable mission checklist (set up direct deposit, complete a quiz, check Credit Builder)
    • Incentives for completing key steps, with a progress UI to visualise completion
    • Smart dialog, Discover feed, in-app and push messages to support the mission flow

  • Why it mattered

    • Helped users understand what MoneyLion offers and where to begin
    • Created momentum and early reward moments
    • Improved confidence through clear guidance

Outcome

Onboarding missions, by the numbers.

Guided missions turned passive sign-ups into active users. Combined with in-app flows and engagement surfaces, they lifted Day 1 engagement by 14% and direct deposit setup by 19%.
