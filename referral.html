Designing the MoneyLion Referral Program

Creating an end-to-end referral experience to drive acquisition, increase retention, and scale through lifecycle comms.

Role
Founding designer
Scope
App, web, email
Company
MoneyLion
Timeline
2021 to 2022
The problem

Get people to invite, and keep inviting.

Goal: build a scalable referral program that motivates existing users to invite friends, engages new users post-signup, and strengthens product adoption through lifecycle touchpoints.

  • 01

    How do we get users to participate, and keep referring?

  • 02

    How do we retain new users once they join?

  • 03

    How do we keep the program fresh and rewarding?

  • 04

    How do we communicate value clearly across product and email?

Strategy

Reward the users already here.

Research showed that existing MoneyLion users were a strong acquisition driver, so we built a referral experience to empower and reward them.

  • Encourage referrers

    • Earn up to $55
    • Festive campaign bonuses
    • Personalised referral post and friend nudges

  • Retain referees

    • Sign-up incentive ($55 via direct deposit)
    • Immediate invitation to refer others
    • Embedded onboarding to MoneyLion products

  • Lifecycle email strategy

    • Existing users: intro → tips → earnings recap
    • New users: welcome → deposit email → intro to referral program
    • Product education woven into email comms
